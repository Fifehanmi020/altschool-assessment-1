My Hobbies

I enjoy getting immersed in different stories, characters, and fictional worlds. Beyond entertainment, I find the creativity involved in storytelling and world-building interesting. Another hobby of mine is designing. Graphic Design is not only a skill I am developing professionally; it is also something I genuinely enjoy doing. I like experimenting with layouts, typography, colours, images, and different visual ideas to see how they can come together to communicate a message. I also enjoy photography and exploring creative ideas. I like observing how images, colours, composition, and details can change the way something is perceived. Exploring technology is another activity I have grown to enjoy, especially now that I am learning Software Engineering. There is something satisfying about discovering how things work and trying to create something from scratch.

Why I joined the program:

As someone who is new to Software Engineering, I have always been interested in becoming a Software Engineer. The thought of being able to develop websites and create other amazing things with technology has always fascinated me. I joined this program to pave the way for a career path I have always wanted to pursue. My goal is to become a confident Fullstack Engineer who can help people solve problems and bring their ideas to life.

My Goals:

My goal is to become a successful Software Engineer who is skilled and confident in his craft. As a fresh graduate, I want to develop a strong technical skill set that will give me a better chance in the job market. I want to become a developer who is reliable, committed to learning, and good at what he does. I also want to develop strong problem-solving skills and be able to build useful solutions that can make a real difference in people’s lives. Most importantly, I want to keep improving, stay open to learning new technologies, and build a career that I can be proud of. Ultimately, I want my skills to be useful to other people. Whether I am designing a visual identity, developing a website, or building a software solution, I want the things I create to have a purpose.
