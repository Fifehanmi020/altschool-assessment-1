BIOGRAPHY

My name is Oye David Oluwafifehanmi, I’m from Ondo State. I studied Agricultural Extension and Rural Development at the Federal University Of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB). I am a Graphic Designer who enjoys creating captivating visuals, I am interested in exploring the world of Tech. My aim is to expand my skill set by becoming a Software Engineer.

My Interests

I have a strong interest in technology, particularly in areas where creativity and problem-solving come together. My background in Graphic Design has made me interested in how ideas can be communicated visually, while my growing interest in Software Engineering has introduced me to another way of solving problems and bringing ideas to life. I am especially interested in web development and the process of building useful digital products from an idea. I am also interested in learning how technology works beyond just using it. I want to understand how websites and applications are built, how different parts of a system communicate, and how developers turn problems into practical solutions. One of the things that excites me about Software Engineering is that there is always something new to learn, which gives me the opportunity to keep improving and challenging myself.

My Vision

My vision is to build a career that allows me to combine creativity, technology, and problem-solving. I do not want to limit myself to being good at only one thing. I want to develop both my creative abilities as a Graphic Designer and my technical abilities as a Software Engineer. In the next few years, I hope to become a confident Fullstack Engineer capable of building useful and reliable digital products. I want to be able to take an idea, understand the problem behind it, and contribute to turning that idea into a working solution. I also want to become someone who is constantly improving. Technology changes quickly, so I understand that becoming a Software Engineer is not something that ends when I complete a course or get my first job. I want to keep learning, exploring new technologies, building projects, and developing better ways of solving problems.
